# Panda artwork prompts

These project assets were created with the built-in ImageGen tool. The PNGs are enlarged raster
masters inspired by polished 64×64 sprites, not native 64×64 exports. The mascot was generated
first; it was then used as the identity reference for the logo and banner.

## Mascot

Output: `mascot.png`. Transparent background. Generated without a reference image.

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: final original mascot sprite for Panda, a local Ethereum development environment with real execution clients, consensus clients and validators. This panda refers to Ethereum's Merge symbolism. Create an original character, not a reproduction of an existing logo.
Primary request: one compact black-and-white panda, seated in a calm, capable pose, shown in subtle three-quarter view. Refined, minimalist retro pixel art in the visual language of a professionally hand-crafted 64x64 game sprite.
Subject: a recognizable adult-proportioned stylized panda with rounded ears, clear black eye patches, a small dark nose, ivory muzzle and belly, charcoal shoulder band and simple rounded paws resting naturally. Head about 40 percent of the total character height; compact body, believable silhouette, restrained friendly expression. Two small visible eyes with tiny controlled highlights. No exaggerated baby head or anime eyes. The face and paws should be readable at small size.
Style: polished 16-bit indie-game sprite, deliberate economical pixel clusters on one regular logical grid, crisp square pixel edges, carefully stepped contours, selective thin dark outline, two controlled shadow levels. Show as an enlarged nearest-neighbor sprite. Minimal detail, quiet asymmetry, no visual noise. The character should feel like a thoughtfully designed developer-tool mascot.
Palette: approximately 8 solid colors: charcoal #17212B, deep blue-gray #304451, muted cool gray #889DA6, soft gray #C5D0D2, warm ivory #F2F1E8 and off-white #FCFCF7; a tiny restrained cyan reflected-light accent #79BDCA is optional. Black and white dominate.
Composition: centered full-body character occupying about 65 to 70 percent of a square canvas; generous transparent margin. Exactly one panda, no other objects.
Text: none. No letters, numbers, wordmark or monogram anywhere.
Constraints: genuinely transparent background. No painted checkerboard, no floor or cast shadow, no scenery, no bamboo, no tools, no clothing, no helmet, no clocks, no lightning, no coins, no Ethereum emblem pasted on the body, no spark mascot. No smooth vector rendering, gradients, soft glow, brush texture, photorealism, 3D render, watermark or fuzzy antialiasing. Keep the silhouette minimal and the pixel craftsmanship precise.
```

## Logo

Output: `logo.png`. Transparent background. Reference: `mascot.png`.

```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: final symbol-only logo for Panda, a local Ethereum development environment. Square image, genuinely transparent background.
Input image 1: the approved Panda mascot, identity reference.
Primary request: derive a compact panda-head emblem from this exact character. Preserve its rounded charcoal ears, ivory face, dark eye patches, tiny nose, restrained friendly expression and subtle three-quarter orientation. The logo should unmistakably belong to the same mascot, while simplifying it for small sizes.
Style: refined 16-bit pixel art on a consistent 32x32 to 64x64 logical sprite grid, shown as a crisp nearest-neighbor enlargement. Deliberately shaped square pixel clusters, carefully stepped outline, broad readable eye patches. Use five to six flat colors: charcoal, dark blue-gray, cool gray, ivory and off-white. One shadow level is enough. Remove detailed fur, individual cheek highlights, whiskers and decorative pixels.
Composition: a single panda head and ears, no body, centered on the square canvas, roughly 65 percent of canvas width, with generous transparent margins. Clear silhouette on both light and dark surfaces; a restrained cool-gray outer edge can separate the darkest fur on dark backgrounds.
Text: none. No letters, initials, numbers, wordmark or monogram. In particular no Z or lightning symbol.
Constraints: preserve this panda's identity; create an original pixel-art mark. No shield, circle, badge, frame, bamboo, tools, ornaments or background. No drawn checkerboard, soft shadow, gradients, blur, glossy reflections, 3D, smooth vector paths or watermark. Professional minimal pixel craftsmanship, not an emoji.
```

## Banner

Output: `banner.png`. Opaque background. Reference: `mascot.png`.

```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: minimalist README brand banner for Panda, a local Ethereum development environment with real execution and consensus clients and validators. Wide 3:1 landscape PNG, opaque background.
Input image 1: the approved Panda mascot, identity reference. Preserve its exact seated pose, facial features, ears, eye patches, paws, proportions and restrained charcoal/ivory/cool-gray pixel palette.
Primary request: a polished, quiet retro pixel-art brand lockup. Place the full seated panda on the left of a custom pixel wordmark reading exactly "Panda". The character and wordmark together form a balanced centered horizontal composition.
Layout: generous negative space on every side. The combined group occupies about 65 percent of the banner width. Panda height is about 60 percent of the banner height. The wordmark is about 30 percent of the banner height and aligned optically with the panda's center. Clear generous gap between the panda and lettering. No other scene elements.
Style: refined 16-bit pixel art, crisp regular square pixel grid, intentional economical clusters. Preserve the mascot's quality and simplify only incidental tiny details as needed for the wide layout. Lettering should have an elegant substantial stepped pixel shape with consistent strokes and carefully spaced characters; readable first, quietly playful second.
Text (verbatim): "Panda" — capital P, lowercase a n d a. Exactly once, perfectly spelled. Warm ivory #F2F1E8 text. No slogan, subtitle, extra label, initials or letters elsewhere.
Background: one flat opaque deep navy #142333. Keep the panda readable with its existing cool-gray edge shading; do not add a halo, spotlight or glow.
Constraints: original project brand artwork. One panda, one wordmark, nothing else. No blockchain symbols, clocks, lightning, insects, bamboo, tools, hats, gears, coins, particles, networks of lines, texture, vignette, gradient, mockup, border, cards, 3D render, watermark or antialiasing. Keep the layout minimal, deliberate and professionally finished.
```
