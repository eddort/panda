# Artwork prompts

The zap-net artwork was created with the built-in ImageGen tool. The PNGs are enlarged raster
masters inspired by polished 64×64 pixel-art sprites; they are not native 64×64 exports.

The initial mascot concept was refined into the final Iskrovik sprite. The final mascot then served
as the image reference for both the logo and banner. The logo and banner contain no lettering or
Z-shaped monogram.

## Initial mascot concept

```text
Use case: illustration-story.
Asset type: final original pixel-art mascot for zap-net, designed in the visual language of a polished 64x64 game sprite, enlarged for inspection. Transparent background.
Primary request: "Iskrovik", a compact living electrical spark. It embodies a deliberate pulse that advances a real local blockchain. It should feel quietly energetic, precise and capable, with a memorable restrained silhouette.
Design: one small floating elemental, not an animal. A compact asymmetric rounded-diamond body made of warm amber electrical energy around a small midnight-blue center. The center contains two short pale-ivory horizontal eyes with a calm focused expression, no mouth. Three short carefully shaped angular energy tips rise from the upper silhouette; a small tapered lower point balances them. One tiny cool cyan edge accent helps distinguish electricity from ordinary fire. No separate head, no limbs, no accessories. The silhouette is a living spark or energized crystal, never a lightning-bolt letter.
Style: expert handcrafted 16-bit pixel art on an approximately 64x64 logical grid. Intentional pixel clusters, elegant 1- and 2-pixel contour steps, selective thin dark outlining, one controlled ivory highlight and two hue-shifted amber shadow levels for a little depth. Minimal detail, clear negative space. Subtle three-quarter volume, asymmetric poised shape. Mature indie-game art direction, not a crude flat emoji and not a childish blob. Crisp visibly square pixels; do not paint smooth textures inside pixel shapes.
Palette: 8 to 10 solid colors based on darkest midnight #142333, dark teal #284553, amber #D4973E, warm yellow #F2C65B, pale ivory #F6E8BE and restrained cyan #86BFC3. No red fire, no purple neon, no rainbow.
Composition: one centered full mascot, occupying roughly 70 percent of the square canvas, generous transparent margin. No detached objects; at most one tiny neighboring square spark if it materially helps.
Text: NONE. No letters of any kind. Especially no Z or z, no Z-shaped body, no Z-shaped marking or lightning monogram, no wordmark, no typography, no number, no rune.
Constraints: truly transparent background; no drawn checkerboard, no backdrop, no floor, no scene, no blur, no soft glow, no gradient, no 3D render, no watermark. No insects, wings, antennae, clocks, watch hands, coins, tools, armor, costume or gears. Keep it simple, distinctive, refined, and recognizably electrical.
```

## Final mascot refinement

Reference: the initial mascot concept.

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit.
Input image 1: current Iskrovik sprite, edit target.
Primary request: refine this character into a compact electrical SPARK, preserving its dark diamond core, two ivory eyes, amber/ivory/cyan palette and polished 64x64-style pixel clusters.
Change only the outer energy silhouette and expression: remove the long upward flame tongues entirely. Replace them with short sharp stepped electrical points around a compact rounded-diamond body. The whole silhouette should now be approximately as wide as it is tall, a poised four-direction spark with a little intentional asymmetry. Outer points should be no longer than one fifth of the core width. Keep four to six deliberate large energy facets, remove small rippling flame details. Make the two eyes short calm horizontal slits rather than aggressive diagonal eyes. Preserve the subtle three-quarter volume and selected thin outline.
Art direction: a controlled living pulse, minimal, precise, professional 16-bit game sprite. Dark core around 35 percent of sprite width, clean open shapes. No ornate framing. Strong small-size readability. Render as an enlarged crisp nearest-neighbor preview, on a regular 64x64-style pixel grid.
Text: none. NO letters, especially no Z or z, no Z-shaped symbol, no alphabetic monogram.
Constraints: keep genuinely transparent background. No fire/flame tongues, no wings, no antennae, no clock, no feet, no tools, no extra objects, no text, no watermark, no smooth painting, no blurred glow. Maintain the same color family and pixel-art finish. Center the new compact sprite with about 15 percent margin on all sides.
```

## Logo

Reference: the final mascot in `mascot.png`.

```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: final standalone symbol-only pixel-art logo for zap-net; square image, transparent background.
Input image 1: Iskrovik mascot, identity reference. Preserve its recognizable four-direction amber electrical-spark silhouette and dark diamond center.
Primary request: make a compact, simplified emblem of this mascot for use as a small logo. A precisely drawn pixel spark: four tapered directional points, a slightly asymmetric upper edge, a dark diamond center and two tiny level ivory eyes. Remove small flame-like nicks, most internal shading and all soft eye glow. Reduce to five or six solid colors: darkest navy, amber, warm yellow, ivory, and one small cyan accent. Retain enough character to match the reference while improving small-size clarity.
Style: expert 16-bit pixel art; economical deliberate clusters, hard square pixel edges, one consistent logical grid, minimal selective outline. The icon should read clearly as a complete 32x32 to 64x64 sprite when reduced. Balanced negative space and refined proportions. Show it as a large nearest-neighbor enlargement.
Composition: only one centered icon, occupying 65 to 70 percent of a square canvas, with generous transparent margins.
Text: absolutely NONE. No lettering or typography anywhere. In particular no Z, z, Z-shaped bolt, letter silhouette, monogram or wordmark. Do not write the project name.
Constraints: genuine transparency, no checkerboard drawn into artwork, no circles or shields behind the emblem, no frame, no scenery, no accessories, no clock, no insects, no blur, no gradient, no shine effect, no smooth vector rendering, no watermark. The form must be a spark, never a disguised letter.
```

## Banner

Reference: the final mascot in `mascot.png`.

```text
Use case: illustration-story.
Asset type: final minimalist README banner for zap-net, wide 3:1 landscape image, opaque dark background.
Input image 1: final Iskrovik mascot, identity reference. Preserve its compact amber four-direction spark shape, dark diamond core, level ivory eyes, restrained cyan edging and crisp 64x64-style pixel art.
Primary request: a quiet pixel-art scene showing one deliberate spark advancing a small blockchain. On a perfectly flat deep midnight #142333 canvas, put the reference mascot in the left third. To its right, place exactly three small clean square block tiles in one horizontal row, connected by a thin understated pixel line. The first tile has a warm amber edge and a tiny ivory pulse at its center, the next two have muted blue-teal edges. This is the entire composition.
Composition: the group of mascot plus three blocks is centered within the banner, occupying about two thirds of its width. The mascot occupies about half the image height; blocks are about one third of mascot height. Generous empty space above, below and at both ends. No panel, no card UI, no fake dashboard. Clear spacing, all elements aligned to a single pixel grid.
Style: premium restrained 16-bit adventure-game art direction, crisp square pixels and sparse intentional clusters. Keep the mascot's existing detail; keep the line and blocks extremely simple. Subtle volume in the mascot only. No soft lighting effects. Limited amber, ivory, dark teal and cyan palette. The scene should feel calm and controlled rather than frantic.
Text: none. No project name, no labels, no numbers, no letters of any kind; especially no Z or z and no Z-shaped monogram. The project name will be normal text outside the banner in the README.
Constraints: preserve mascot identity. Exactly one mascot and three block tiles. No clocks, insects, wings, gears, coins, arrows, stars, particles, swooshes, speed lines, circuit-board background, decorative border, gradient, vignette, blurry glow, texture or watermark. Flat opaque dark background. Keep it minimal and carefully composed.
```
